Meet the team

Join a Meeting is a voice-first website builder. You talk to four AI agents. They plan, design, build, test and publish a plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript site, one after the other.

CEO

Listens, asks what you are building and why, then reads the requirements back until you say yes.

Designer

Chooses layout, colours and fonts with you and hands the Developer a short design brief.

Developer

Writes the site with plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, and fixes what the Tester finds.

Tester

Opens the finished site in a real browser, reviews a screenshot, then deploys to GitHub and Vercel once you confirm.

Your code

Every site the team builds for you is saved here, with the result of its automated test.

Your sites are saved automatically and linked to this browser, so come back on the same one to find them.

How is this saved?

No sign-up is needed. Your workspace is tied to this browser. Using a different browser or device, or clearing this browser's site data, starts a new empty workspace. Use the link above to bring your sites with you.

Deploy

Publish a saved site to a new GitHub repository and Vercel. You will be asked to confirm before anything is created.
